Privacy information

This starter project does not include user accounts or a hosted chat database. Chat history is kept in the current browser session only. Messages sent to the AI endpoint are processed by the API provider configured by the site owner. Do not enter passwords, financial details, private documents or other sensitive information.

Before opening this service to the public, the site owner should publish a complete privacy notice, set retention rules, configure rate limits and review the AI provider's data policies.
